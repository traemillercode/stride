Auto-post settings

When you connect a device, Stride can automatically share your activities to the community feed. Auto-post is off by default — you choose what to share.

Activity types to share

Choose which activity types are auto-posted when you enable device sharing.

Privacy note: Auto-posted activities are visible to your segment community. You can delete any post after it's shared. Stride never shares your data with third parties without your consent.
