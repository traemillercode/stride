Community Guidelines

The rules that keep Stride a place every runner wants to be.

Our principles

Be supportive

Every runner starts somewhere. No harassment, no gatekeeping, no belittling anyone's pace, distance, or experience. Celebrate the person fighting for a 40-minute 5K as loudly as the one chasing a 2:30 marathon.

Be honest

Your PRs matter because they're real. No fabricated race results, no faked Strava activities, no "I totally didn't cut the course" short-cuts. Integrity is the currency of the running community — don't devalue it.

Be safe

Protect yourself and others. Do not share personal contact information, home addresses, or real-time location in public posts. If you're coordinating a meetup, do it in a public place with daylight hours — and remember that Stride is a connection platform, not a chaperone.

Be you

One account, one person. No impersonating other runners, coaches, brands, or public figures. Your training log is yours — own it honestly.

What's not allowed

  • Harassment and bullying — targeted insults, intimidation, or repeated unwanted contact directed at any community member.
  • Hate speech — content that attacks or demeans a group based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, or any protected characteristic.
  • Spam and self-promotion — unsolicited commercial content, affiliate-link dumping, repetitive posting, or using the community purely as a marketing channel.
  • False race results — fabricating, inflating, or misrepresenting race times, distances, or activity data.
  • Sharing others' personal data — posting another person's name, contact info, location, or other identifying details without their consent.
  • Impersonation — pretending to be another runner, coach, brand, or public figure.
  • Encouraging dangerous training practices — promoting disordered eating, running through serious injury, or other advice that a reasonable coach would flag as harmful.

Reporting

If you see something that violates these guidelines, email us at:

moderation@stride.com

We review reports within 48 hours. Depending on the severity and history, possible outcomes include:

  • Warning — a notice explaining what rule was broken and a request to correct it.
  • Content removal — the violating post or comment is taken down.
  • Temporary suspension — access to community features is paused for a set period.
  • Permanent ban — account and access to Stride are permanently revoked.

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you may appeal by replying to the decision notice or emailing the moderation address above. Appeals are reviewed by a different moderator than the original decision-maker.

Moderation

We review all flagged content. We may also proactively moderate community spaces to ensure they stay welcoming. Moderation decisions are final at the time they are made, but are always appealable. We reserve the right to update these guidelines as the community grows — material changes will be announced in-community.
