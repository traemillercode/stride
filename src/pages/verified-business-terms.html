Verified Business Terms

Rules, rights, and responsibilities for clubs, brands, and organizations on Stride.

Who these terms apply to

These Verified Business Terms apply to organizations that apply for and receive verified status on Stride, including:

  • Local run clubs — community-based running groups, track clubs, and social run crews.
  • Running stores — brick-and-mortar and online retailers specializing in running footwear, apparel, and gear.
  • Race organizers — companies and nonprofits that produce road races, trail races, track meets, and multi-sport events.
  • Brands — footwear, apparel, nutrition, and accessory companies serving the running community.
  • Coaching services — individual coaches, coaching collectives, and training platforms.
  • Non-profit running organizations — charities, advocacy groups, and foundations with a running-related mission.

Individual runner accounts are governed by the Terms of Service, not these Verified Business Terms. If you're an individual runner, these terms do not apply to you.

Verification requirements

To apply for verified status, an organization must provide:

  • Legal business name — the registered legal name of the organization, not a nickname or informal title.
  • Physical address — a valid street address associated with the organization.
  • Contact email — an email address monitored by someone authorized to represent the organization.
  • Proof of status — business registration certificate, tax ID (EIN or equivalent), or comparable documentation establishing the organization as a legitimate entity.
  • Primary contact person — the name and role of an individual authorized to manage the organization's Stride presence.

Stride reserves the right to approve or deny verification at its sole discretion. We may request additional documentation if the initial submission is insufficient. Verification review typically takes 5–10 business days. We'll notify you at the contact email provided when a decision is made.

Verified account privileges

Once verified, your organization receives the following privileges on Stride:

  • Verified badge — a verified checkmark displayed on your organization's profile, signaling authenticity to the Stride community.
  • Public circles — the ability to create and manage community circles visible to all Stride users.
  • Event posting — the ability to create and publish events, including group runs, races, clinics, and meetups.
  • Product and service listings — in future releases, the ability to list products or services on your profile (feature not yet available).
  • Priority support — access to a prioritized support queue for verified business inquiries.

These privileges may be modified, expanded, or revoked at any time. Revocation may occur if these terms, the Community Guidelines, or the Terms of Service are violated.

Trademark and branding

Verified businesses may reference Stride by name to describe their presence on the platform — for example, "Find us on Stride" or "Join our Stride community."

Verified businesses may not use Stride's logo, brand mark (runner silhouette), wordmark, or any other Stride-owned visual assets without prior written permission. This includes use on merchandise, marketing materials, websites, social media, or event collateral.

By applying for and accepting verified status, your organization grants Stride a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your organization's name and logo within the Stride platform for discovery and identification purposes — for example, in search results, your verified profile, and event listings. This license is limited to use within Stride and does not grant Stride any ownership of your marks.

Content and conduct

Verified businesses must comply with the Community Guidelines at all times. In addition:

  • No spam — do not post repetitive, unsolicited promotional content across circles, feeds, or direct messages.
  • No false claims — all statements about products, services, pricing, availability, and endorsements must be truthful and not misleading.
  • Accurate events — events posted through Stride must be real events with accurate dates, times, locations, and details. Cancelled or rescheduled events must be updated promptly.
  • User-generated content — businesses are responsible for content posted by users within their circles. Businesses must moderate their circles to ensure compliance with the Community Guidelines.
  • No misleading pricing — if pricing is mentioned, it must be current, accurate, and inclusive of any mandatory fees. "Bait and switch" pricing is prohibited.

Liability

Verified businesses are solely responsible for all events, activities, meetups, and interactions they organize, promote, or facilitate through Stride. This includes but is not limited to group runs, races, training sessions, clinics, product demos, and social gatherings.

Stride is not liable for any injuries, damages, losses, or disputes arising from or related to business-organized activities. This includes, without limitation, personal injury, property damage, financial loss, and disputes between businesses and participants.

Verified businesses must carry their own liability insurance appropriate to the nature and scale of their activities. Stride does not provide insurance coverage for business-organized events.

Termination

Either party may terminate the verified business relationship at any time, with or without cause, by providing notice to the other party.

Upon termination:

  • The verified badge is removed from your organization's profile.
  • Any circles you created become read-only — existing content remains visible but new posts and interactions are disabled.
  • All events you published are unpublished and removed from the Stride event directory.
  • The limited license to display your organization's name and logo terminates, and both parties will cease use of the other's marks except as necessary to reflect historical content (e.g., past event listings).

Stride may immediately suspend or terminate a verified account without prior notice if the business violates these terms, the Community Guidelines, or the Terms of Service, or if the business's continued presence on Stride poses a safety risk to the community.

Changes to these terms

We will notify verified businesses of material changes to these Verified Business Terms at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Notice will be sent to the contact email associated with your verified account. Continued use of verified account features after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. If you do not agree with the changes, you may terminate your verified status before they take effect.
