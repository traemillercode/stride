How the pros train

Real routines from real runners

Conner Mantz

Two-time US Olympic marathoner, 2024 Chicago Marathon top American finisher

Mantz runs 130–140 miles per week during marathon builds, with his key workout being a 22-mile long run that finishes at marathon pace. His approach: "I'd rather be slightly undertrained than overtrained." He takes an easy day after every hard session — no exceptions.

via Runner's World interview, January 2025
