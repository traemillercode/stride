The Split

What's happening across the running world

Road

Mantz top American at Olympic marathon

Conner Mantz placed 8th at the 2024 Paris Olympic marathon in 2:08:12 — the top American finish. In a Runner's World profile, Mantz shared his training philosophy: 130-mile weeks anchored by a 22-mile long run at marathon pace, with an easy day after every hard session — no exceptions.

via Runner's World, Aug 2024

Track

Kipyegon shatters 1500m world record

Faith Kipyegon broke her own women's 1500m world record at the 2024 Paris Diamond League, clocking 3:49.04 to become the first woman under 3:50. The three-time Olympic champion now holds the 1500m, mile, and 5000m world records simultaneously.

via World Athletics, Jul 2024

Read more →
Trail

Walmsley and Schide dominate UTMB

Jim Walmsley and Katie Schide claimed decisive victories at UTMB 2024 in Chamonix. Walmsley defended his 2023 title while Schide broke the women's course record by over 20 minutes on the 106-mile Alpine loop, cementing both as the era's top ultra-distance racers.

via iRunFar, Sep 2024

Nike launches Alphafly 4

Nike released the Alphafly 4 with a redesigned Air Zoom unit and an updated Atomknit upper. Early reviews call it the most stable version of the supershoe yet, with improved cornering and a wider forefoot platform — a meaningful shift for the marathon racing category.
