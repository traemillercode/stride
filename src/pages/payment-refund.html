Payment & Refund Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

1. What you're paying for

Stride may offer the following paid services, all delivered digitally:

  • NIL sponsorship subscriptions — recurring contributions that support a specific runner's training and competition expenses through our name, image, and likeness sponsorship module.
  • Pro athlete support contributions — one-time contributions to a professional athlete's training fund.
  • Premium features — future subscription-based features including advanced training analytics, personalized coaching tools, and exclusive community spaces.

All paid services are digital in nature. No physical goods are shipped.

2. Pricing

Pricing for each service is displayed at checkout before you complete payment. All prices are in US dollars (USD). We reserve the right to change pricing with reasonable notice to active subscribers. Price changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Stride does not store full credit card numbers on our servers. By making a payment, you also agree to Stripe's terms of service, available at stripe.com/legal.

4. Refunds

Our refund policy varies by purchase type:

  • Subscriptions (including NIL sponsorship) — you have a 14-day refund window from the date of payment for the unused portion of your subscription. If you cancel and request a refund within 14 days, we'll refund the most recent payment. After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion.
  • One-time contributions (pro athlete support) — these are non-refundable unless there was a processing error (e.g., duplicate charge, incorrect amount). If an error occurred, contact us immediately.
  • Premium features — subject to the same 14-day refund policy as subscriptions. If you used the feature substantially during the refund window, refunds are at our discretion.

To request a refund, email:

support@stride.com

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and returned to the original payment method.

5. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer (a "chargeback") rather than contacting us first, your account may be temporarily suspended pending resolution of the dispute. We encourage you to reach out to support@stride.com before initiating a chargeback — we want to make it right.

6. Cancellation

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain access to paid features until that date. We do not offer prorated refunds for partial months when cancelling after the 14-day refund window.

7. Taxes

Applicable sales tax is calculated and added at checkout based on your billing address. Tax rates vary by jurisdiction. You are responsible for any additional taxes that may apply in your location.
