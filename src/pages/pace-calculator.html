VDOT is a measure of running fitness developed by Dr. Jack Daniels, published in Daniels' Running Formula. It lets you derive equivalent performances and training paces from a single race result.

Methodology

Equivalent race times use the Riegel formula (T₂ = T₁ × (D₂/D₁)^1.06), an open predictive model published by Peter Riegel in 1977 and widely used across running science.

Training paces are calculated using VDOT-based methodology from Jack Daniels' Daniels' Running Formula — a publicly documented system that derives training intensities from a single race performance. Zone ranges are approximate; individual physiology varies.

For another perspective on your training zones, compare with McMillan Running's calculator — a respected independent resource.
