Data deletion

How to request that your data be deleted from Stride

What "data deletion" means at Stride

When you request data deletion, we remove your personal information from our systems. This is a permanent, irreversible action. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • Connected device data from Garmin, Strava, Coros, Apple Health, Amazfit, and Suunto
  • Your full training history — workouts, mileage logs, race results, and personal records
  • Community posts, comments, and shared routes you've created
  • Your training roadmaps and plan data
  • Any personal identifiers (name, email, profile photo)

What may be retained

  • Anonymized aggregate stats. Metrics like "average pace across all users" or "total community mileage" are derived from aggregated data and cannot be tied back to individual accounts. These may be retained indefinitely for community features and platform analytics.
  • Legal and security obligations. We may retain certain records (e.g., payment receipts, security logs) as required by law or to defend against legal claims. These records are retained only for the legally required period and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Backup archives. Encrypted backups may retain your data for up to 90 days after deletion before being rotated out. These backups are never used to restore deleted accounts.

How to request deletion

Stride's deletion process is currently manual. We're building an automated self-service pipeline, but right now a human on our team handles every request.

To request data deletion, email us with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Stride account. This is how we verify you own the account.

support@stride.com

Subject line: Data Deletion Request

What happens next

  1. Confirmation. You'll receive a confirmation email within 3 business days verifying we received your request. If you don't see it, check your spam folder or resend.
  2. Verification. We may ask for additional proof of account ownership before proceeding with deletion.
  3. Deletion. Once verified, your data is removed within 30 days. You'll receive a final confirmation when deletion is complete.
  4. Backup expiry. Encrypted backup copies cycle out within 90 days.

Before you delete

  • Data deletion is permanent. We cannot recover your account, training history, or community posts after deletion.
  • If you'd like a copy of your data first, mention that in your email and we'll export it in a machine-readable format before deletion begins.
  • Active subscriptions: if you have a paid Stride plan, cancel it first through your account settings or your payment provider. Deletion does not automatically cancel billing.
  • Third-party devices: revoking Stride's access in Garmin Connect, Strava, or your other device platforms is a separate step — we recommend doing it alongside your deletion request for a clean break.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have additional rights around your data — including access, correction, portability, and the right to withdraw consent. See our Privacy Policy for the full list and how to exercise them.
