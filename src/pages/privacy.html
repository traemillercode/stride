Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

1. What we collect

Stride collects the following categories of data to provide and improve our running community and training platform:

  • Account information — name, email address, and profile details you provide when creating an account.
  • Device and health data — when you connect a device (Garmin, Strava, Coros, Apple Health, Amazfit, or Suunto), we collect activity metrics including distance, pace, heart rate, elevation, cadence, and GPS routes. This data is pulled via each platform's official API with your explicit authorization.
  • Training metrics — mileage, pace targets, race goals, injury history (self-reported during onboarding), and workout completion data.
  • Location data — city-level location derived from your device data and profile for race discovery and community matching. We do not track real-time location.
  • Payment information — processed through PCI-compliant third-party payment processors. Stride does not store full credit card numbers.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and interaction patterns for improving the platform.

2. How we use your data

We use collected data for the following purposes:

  • Roadmap and training plan construction — your pace, mileage history, injury background, and goals inform the periodized training plans built by our platform.
  • Community features — shared training metrics and activity feeds within community spaces you choose to join.
  • Race projections — pace and distance data feed our field-projection tool to estimate race finish times.
  • Platform improvement — aggregated, anonymized usage patterns help us refine features and fix issues.
  • Communication — service updates, plan notifications, and optional community digests. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

3. Third-party sharing

We share data only as necessary to operate the platform:

  • Payment processors — for billing and subscription management (e.g., Stripe).
  • Affiliate networks — when you click through to gear retailers via our resource pages, standard affiliate tracking cookies may be set by the retailer. See our Cookie Policy for details.
  • Analytics providers — anonymized usage data for understanding platform performance.
  • Moderation services — community content may be scanned by automated moderation APIs to enforce our community guidelines.
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose data when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Correction — update or correct inaccurate data.
  • Opt out — withdraw consent for non-essential data processing, revoke device connections, or delete your account at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days (45 days for complex requests under GDPR).

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the platform. Training data and community posts are retained until account deletion. Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytics purposes. Payment records are retained per legal requirements.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encryption in transit (TLS), encrypted storage at rest, and access controls — to protect your data. No system is impenetrable; in the event of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected users within 72 hours as required by applicable law.

7. Children

Stride is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

8. International transfers

Stride operates in the United States. If you access the platform from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US. We rely on standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards for international data transfers where required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We will notify users of material changes via email or a prominent notice on the platform. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, contact us at:

privacy@stride.run
