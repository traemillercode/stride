Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help sites remember your preferences, understand how you use them, and provide certain features. Cookies can be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (retained for a set period).

How Stride uses cookies

Stride uses cookies minimally and purposefully. We do not use cookies for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

Functional cookies (required)

These are essential for the platform to work. They handle session management (keeping you logged in), form state (remembering your onboarding progress), and your cookie consent preference. Stride cannot function without these cookies.

Analytics cookies

We use privacy-focused analytics (self-hosted, no third-party scripts) to understand which features are used most and where the platform needs improvement. These cookies collect anonymized data about page visits and feature interactions. No personal data is transmitted to third-party analytics providers.

Affiliate tracking cookies

When you click through to a gear retailer from our Resource pages, the retailer may set its own cookies to attribute the referral. These are third-party cookies set by the retailer's domain, not by Stride. Stride does not read or access these cookies — they are governed by the retailer's own privacy policy. We link to retailers transparently and only from our gear and resource pages.

Managing your preferences

You control cookie settings in several ways:

  • Browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Instructions vary by browser: consult your browser's help documentation. Blocking all cookies may prevent Stride's core features from working.
  • Cookie consent banner. When you first visit Stride, our consent banner lets you accept all cookies or navigate to this page to learn more before deciding.
  • Clear consent. You can reset your cookie preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for Stride (specifically, the cookieConsent key) and reloading the page.
  • Affiliate links. To avoid affiliate cookies, navigate directly to retailers rather than clicking through from Stride.

Third-party services

Stride embeds content from Spotify (Web Playback SDK), Apple Music (MusicKit JS), and YouTube (IFrame Player API) for treadmill companion and playlist features. These embeds may set their own cookies governed by each platform's privacy policy. Stride does not control these cookies.

Updates to this policy

We update this policy when our cookie practices change. Check back periodically; we'll note the effective date at the top of this page. For material changes, we'll alert you via the platform.
